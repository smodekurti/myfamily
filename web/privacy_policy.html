Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 31, 2025

MyFamily ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by MyFamily.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Personal Information: Includes your name, email address, profile picture (optional), and date of birth (optional) provided during registration.
  • Family Content: Information you generate, such as tasks, grocery lists, events, chat messages, and photos uploaded to the app.
  • Device Information: Information about your mobile device, including model, operating system, and push notification tokens.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Provision: To create your account, manage your family groups, and sync data across devices.
  • Communication: To send you important notifications (e.g., new tasks, chat messages) via Push Notifications.
  • Authentication: To verify your identity using Third-Party services like Google or Apple Sign-In.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our application:

  • Supabase: Used for secure user authentication, database storage, and file storage.
  • Firebase (Google): Used for Push Notifications (FCM) and Google Sign-In.
  • Apple: Used for Sign-In with Apple.
  • Open-Meteo: Used to display weather information based on your location (if granted).

4. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Camera & Photo Library: To allow you to upload profile pictures or share photos in chat.
  • Notifications: To alert you about family activities.
  • FaceID / Biometrics: To securely unlock the app locally on your device.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data as long as you use our service. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

To delete your account: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account within the app. This action is irreversible and will remove your personal data from our servers immediately (or within 30 days for backups).
